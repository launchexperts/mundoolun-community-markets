Market-Day Logistics

How a Mundoolun Sunday runs

Plan your day with confidence — these times are fixed.

4:00am — Bump In

Stallholders can begin setting up from 4:00am. We'll guide you to your spot on arrival.

6:45am — Vehicles Cleared

All vehicles must be removed from the market stall area by 6:45am — no exceptions, for visitor safety.

7:00am – 1:00pm — Trading

Doors open to the public at 7:00am. Trade through to 1:00pm, then bump out.

Requirements

What you'll need to trade with us

To keep the markets safe, professional and council-compliant, every stallholder needs to meet these requirements before trading.

All Stallholders

$20 million Public Liability Insurance. A current certificate of currency must be provided with your application.

Food Trucks & Food Stalls

In addition to PLI, food vendors must hold:

A current Food Licence issued by the relevant local authority.

A permit to operate at Mundoolun Park from Logan City Council. We can help point you to the right council contact during the application process.

Stall Fees

Standard stall fee: $30 per market day (3m × 3m site, indicative — confirmed at application). Food trucks and larger sites quoted on application.

We don't lock you into a season — pay per market.

Stallholder FAQs

Common questions

How much does a stall cost?

Standard 3m × 3m stalls are $30 per market day (indicative — confirmed at application). Larger sites and food trucks are quoted individually. There's no season lock-in — pay per market.

Do I need Public Liability Insurance?

Yes — $20 million Public Liability Insurance is required for every stallholder. You'll need to provide a current certificate of currency with your application.

What do food trucks need?

In addition to PLI, food trucks need a current Food Licence and a permit to operate at Mundoolun Park from Logan City Council. We can point you to the right council contact when you apply.

When can I bump in?

Setup begins at 4:00am. All stallholder vehicles must be cleared from the market area by 6:45am — no exceptions, for visitor safety. Doors open at 7:00am.

Can I bring my own marquee and trestle?

Yes, absolutely. If you don't have your own, marquee and trestle hire is available on the day at additional cost — we'll include options in your booking confirmation.

What if the weather is bad?

We run rain, hail or shine. In the rare event of severe weather, we'll make a postponement or cancellation call by 4:00pm the day before and contact all booked stallholders directly.

How do I get approved?

Submit the enquiry form on this page. We aim to respond within a few days. You'll be asked to upload your PLI certificate (and Food Licence + Council permit if applicable) before your first market.

Ready to set up shop?

The first market is Sunday 5 July 2026. The earlier you apply, the easier we can plan your spot.
