Our Story

A market grown by the community, for the community.

The Mundoolun Community Markets began as a handful of trestle tables under the old fig tree — a place for neighbours to swap backyard produce and a good yarn. Today it's a thriving monthly gathering that champions local farmers, artisans and small food makers from across the Scenic Rim.

Every dollar spent here stays close to home, and every stall tells a story. We're proudly run by volunteers, with profits returned to local schools and landcare projects.

What's On

From dawn-picked produce to live tunes on the green — here's a taste of what you'll find when you wander in.
