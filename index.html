Our Story

A market grown by the community, for the community.

Launching Sunday 5 July 2026, the Mundoolun Community Markets is a brand-new monthly gathering on the first Sunday of every month — bringing together local growers, makers, plant nurseries and food trucks from across the Scenic Rim.

It's a privately-run venture with big ambitions: a 100+ stall market that becomes the place locals plan their Sunday morning around. Every stall tells a story, and every dollar spent here stays close to home.

What's On

Something for every basket

From dawn-picked produce to live tunes on the green — here's a taste of what you'll find when you wander in.

Fresh Produce

Seasonal fruit, veg, eggs and honey, picked by the growers who'll be there to greet you.

Handmade & Craft

Pottery, woodwork, soaps, textiles and gifts — all made by hand in the local area.

Food & Coffee

Wood-fired pizza, fresh bakes, preserves and a proper cup of coffee to start the morning.

Live Music & Kids

Local musicians, a petting corner and a craft tent to keep the little ones happily busy.

Become a Stallholder

We're actively seeking stallholders for our launch.

We're building something big — a 100+ stall monthly market on the first Sunday of every month. For our launch on Sunday 5 July 2026, we're especially looking for:

  • Fresh produce stalls
  • Plant & garden stalls
  • Food trucks

What you'll need

All stallholders must hold $20 million Public Liability Insurance.

Food trucks also require a current Food Licence and a permit to operate at Mundoolun Park from Logan City Council.
