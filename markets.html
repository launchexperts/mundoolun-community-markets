What to Expect

The Mundoolun morning

Every market day follows the same easy rhythm — here's how it works.

7:00am — Doors Open

Coffee on, baskets ready. Beat the crowds for the best pick of produce.

9:00am — Live Music

Local musicians on the green, kids' craft tent open, food trucks firing.

1:00pm — Pack Down

Stallholders bump out, the park is back to itself by mid-afternoon.

Weather Policy

Rain, hail or shine (well — mostly shine).

We run rain, hail or shine, but in the rare event of severe weather we may postpone or cancel for safety. We'll always make the call by 4:00pm the day before and announce it on Facebook, Instagram and to our newsletter subscribers.
